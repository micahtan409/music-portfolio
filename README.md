# Music Portfolio | Performance, Composition & Production

Welcome to my music portfolio! I'm a musician and producer who enjoys exploring music through performance, composition, and sound. Music has been a part of my life since I was young, and over the years, I've found different ways to express my creativity through the instruments I play and the music I create.

My background includes percussion, drumset, piano, and mallet instruments, along with experience playing guitar and bass. I've performed in school ensembles, jazz groups, and competitive marching and percussion settings, including Blue Devils C, where I performed marimba.

Outside of performing, I enjoy creating original music in FL Studio. I'm especially interested in jazz, R&B, neo-soul, and chill music, and I like experimenting with chord progressions, melodies, rhythms, and grooves to find my own sound.

This portfolio brings together selected performances, original compositions, music production projects, and experiences that have helped me grow as a musician.
